# HR Attrition & Workforce Analysis

## Project Overview

Analyzed employee attrition and workforce patterns using Excel on 1,470 employee records.

The analysis focuses on identifying employee groups and organizational factors associated with higher attrition.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- XLOOKUP
- SUMIFS
- AVERAGEIFS
- PivotTables
- Conditional Formatting
- Excel Charts

## Analysis Performed

- Cleaned and prepared employee data
- Created employee attrition KPIs
- Calculated attrition rates
- Analyzed attrition by department
- Analyzed attrition by age band
- Analyzed attrition by tenure band
- Analyzed attrition by salary band
- Analyzed attrition by overtime status
- Built an Excel dashboard
- Identified high-risk employee segments

## Key Findings

- Overall employee attrition was 16.1%.
- Sales had the highest department attrition at 20.6%.
- Employees under 25 had 39.2% attrition.
- Employees with less than 2 years of tenure had 34.9% attrition.
- Employees working overtime had 30.5% attrition compared with 10.4% for employees without overtime.
- Sales Representatives with less than 2 years of tenure and overtime showed 87.5% observed attrition.

## Dashboard

The Excel dashboard summarizes workforce size, employee exits, overall attrition and attrition patterns across key employee segments.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate retention challenges within Sales.
- Review onboarding and early-tenure employee experience.
- Examine workload and overtime patterns.
- Prioritize retention strategies for high-risk employee segments.
